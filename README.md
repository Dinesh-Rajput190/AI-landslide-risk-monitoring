# 🌄 AI-Based Early Warning & Landslide Risk Monitoring System in NER

An AI-powered software platform for **early landslide risk prediction, monitoring, GIS-based visualization, and location-based alerts** for the North-Eastern Region (NER) of India.

## 📌 Problem Statement

The North-Eastern Region of India is highly vulnerable to landslides due to heavy rainfall, steep terrain, fragile geology, and human activities such as unplanned hill cutting.

Existing monitoring systems may rely on periodic observations and separate data sources, creating a need for an integrated software solution that can analyze multiple data sources and provide early risk information.

**SIH Problem Statement ID:** SIH26001  
**Theme:** Disaster Management  
**Category:** Software

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Our system integrates historical landslide data, rainfall/weather information, GIS and satellite-derived data with an AI/ML model to estimate landslide risk.

### Key Features

- 🌧️ Rainfall and weather data integration
- 🗺️ GIS-based landslide risk mapping
- 🤖 AI/ML-based risk prediction
- 📊 Real-time monitoring dashboard
- 🚨 Early warning and location-based alerts
- 📍 Risk classification: Low, Moderate, High & Critical
- 🔎 Explainable AI for understanding major risk factors
- 📈 Historical data analysis
- 🌐 Scalable architecture for NER districts
